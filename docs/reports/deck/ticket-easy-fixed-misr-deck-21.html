Disposition rules & common flow Appendix · Proposed, not yet confirmed

AUTOMATE

Low-risk FAQ, a secure status lookup or an approved routine action after verification and policy checks.

HANDOFF

A trained person owns clinical, financial, insurance, identity or legal decisions.

DENY

Refused before any tool is called, with a legitimate route offered.

Receive → Understand → Verify → Ground → Enforce policy → Act or route → Close

Always hand off

medical emergency · claim rejection or appeal · payment fraud or dispute · identity mismatch · key compromise · legal validity · policy conflict · repeated failed verification · the user asks for a human
